Camilo — Founder, Automate305

Miami since age seven. Pro skater. Working DJ. Licensed mold assessor in the Tri-County. I don't have one lane, I have momentum.

My dad was building billing systems on a DOS screen in the late 80s. Green text, black screen. I grew up staring at it not knowing what I was looking at. I just knew it mattered.

FIU. Tech. Nine years using AI before it was a LinkedIn trend. When ChatGPT dropped, I went straight into the rabbit hole and never came back out.

Then I lost my job and became a full-time caregiver for my mom. Early dementia. She lives with me. I built Automate305 in the middle of all of it. Not from a clean business plan. Out of pure necessity. A system that could run while I was in the other room.

It worked. And it kept working.

What to Expect

What Happens on the Audit Call?

No sales pressure. No pitch deck. Just a real conversation about your business.

1.

You tell Camilo how your business runs

Walk him through your current workflow — how leads come in, how you follow up, how you schedule jobs.

2.

He identifies where revenue is leaking

Using your numbers, Camilo maps out the exact points where you're losing money to missed calls, slow follow-up, or manual processes.

3.

You see a custom automation plan

Camilo shows you what an automation system would look like for your specific business — what it would do, what it would cost, what you'd recover.

4.

You decide if you want to move forward

No pressure. The audit is valuable on its own. If the numbers make sense and you want to build, great. If not, you still leave with useful insights.
