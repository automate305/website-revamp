What We Build

Our Three Core Automation Products

Each product targets a different revenue leak — together they form a complete automated revenue recovery system.

🎙️

AI Voice Receptionist

Handles after-hours calls, qualifies leads, and books appointments — 24/7, no hold music, no missed calls. Navi answers in seconds, asks the right questions, and either books the job or routes the lead to the right technician. Most HVAC companies lose 30-40% of their calls after 6pm. We capture every one.

  • Answers calls in under 3 seconds, any time of day
  • Qualifies leads with custom questions for your service area
  • Books directly into your scheduling system
  • Escalates emergencies to on-call techs instantly
📬

Lead Follow-Up Automation

Every new lead gets a follow-up within 2 minutes — not 2 days. Whether they came in through your website, a missed call, or a referral, the system reaches out instantly with a personalized SMS and email sequence that feels human but runs itself. Studies show the first company to respond wins the job 70% of the time.

  • 2-minute response time on every new lead, 24/7
  • Multi-touch sequences: SMS, email, and follow-up calls
  • Automatically tracks lead stage and next action
  • Notifies you only when a lead is ready to book
🗓️

Dispatch & Scheduling Optimization

AI-optimized scheduling reduces drive time and increases daily jobs completed. Your techs get routed smarter — fewer miles, more jobs, less overtime. The system considers zones, job duration, tech skill level, and traffic patterns to fill your calendar efficiently.

  • Zone-based routing that cuts drive time by up to 30%
  • Technician skill matching for faster job completion
  • Automatic customer confirmation and reminder messages
  • Real-time reschedule handling without manual intervention
